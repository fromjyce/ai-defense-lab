Sandbox only. Every score and verification on this page runs against our own in-process detector and our own localhost mock payment API. Nothing here connects to a live payment system, real cardholder data, or any third party.

Evasion curve — attacker vs. detector across retraining generations

Each loop generation: the attacker evolves against the current detector, evasions are mined and folded back into training, the detector retrains, then is re-scored on a fixed, never-mined clean holdout. Watch both lines — a detector that "wins" by degrading into a blanket blocker would show clean PR-AUC collapsing too.

Within-attack evolution

Inside a single loop generation, the evolutionary attacker mutates a population of known-fraud transactions across generations against a fixed detector. This is the fitness-improves-with-generations curve.
